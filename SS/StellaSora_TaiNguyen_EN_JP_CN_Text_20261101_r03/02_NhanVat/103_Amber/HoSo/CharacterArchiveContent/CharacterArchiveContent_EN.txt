Kingdom of Cats I

[CharacterArchiveContent.10311.2]
On Amber's birthday,
she saved up enough money to treat herself to a cat cafe special in the Emor Ash District.

That night, Amber had a dream,
in which she turned into a cat.

As it turned out, through the eyes of a cat, humans were huge and terrifying creatures.
The black cat named Amber tried to control her clumsy limbs as she arched her back, trying to dodge the bustling crowd. Every encounter felt like a brush with death.
Stress made all the fur on her body stand on end. Even if someone did notice this black cat at their feet, they'd probably just mistake her for a stray cat's fur ball—tumbling around the dirty, muddy ground of the Ash District, blown by the winter wind.

"Chirr, meow. (Come here.)" 
Amber shook her left ear—after turning into a cat, her hearing had become exceptionally sharp. She pinpointed the cat's call as coming from a pile of abandoned cardboard boxes across the street.
All she needed to do was cross the street. Then, perhaps, she would be able to find a companion.
Despite this, Amber hesitated when she came to the edge of the street. Even though it was only a few human steps away, in the eyes of a cat, the distance seemed impossibly long, like an unbridgeable ravine.

Suddenly, something lifted her up by the scruff of her neck—before she could react, some creature had grabbed her from behind. The powerful gusts from the giants' legs as they walked brushed past her nose, making her whiskers tremble. Amber the cat, who had been flailing her limbs in panic, instantly stopped struggling.
"Meow. (Hah.)"
A cat's sound came from above, both mocking and comforting at once.
The cat who had picked Amber up deftly traversed the street that looked so intimidating to Amber.

"Meow, purr. (A newbie. Never seen her before.)"
Several round heads poked out from the shaking cardboard box.
"Purr, meow! (Nobody's gonna mess with you, not with us here.)"
The golden head at the bottom meowed twice before a paw pressed it down—a paw which belonged to a purple cat with a tuft of white fur on its forehead.
"Meow! Purr... (Don't talk so big, you runt.)"
The cat's words were aimed at her companion, but her narrow pupils were fixed directly on Amber.

"Meow, meow, purr. (Cat Two, Cat Three, be nice to the newbie.)"
The one who helped Amber finally spoke—she was much larger than the rest of them and seemed to be in charge.
"Meow, meow, purr. (I'm not the one in charge here.)"
As if she'd could read Amber's thoughts, the large cat flicked her tail and jumped up to the ruined staircase above the cardboard box.
...At that moment, Amber realized that even more cats were emerging from the shadows of the stairs, their glowing eyes all fixed on her.

[CharacterArchiveContent.10312.1]
Kingdom of Cats II

[CharacterArchiveContent.10312.2]
At the top of the staircase, a cat sauntered out.
"Meow, meow, purrr. (Newbie, the human world is full of danger. Join our Cat Kingdom Troupe, and we shall keep you safe.)"
She looked down at Amber from above. Her face and ears bore scars big and small from battles.

Troupe... Such a familiar word. Amber tilted her head but couldn't figure out why.
"Meow, meow, purr? (So...the long-haired blue cat who brought me here, is she also from the Cat Kingdom?)" Amber found herself asking in the same language.
"Purr, meow! Purr, meow! (Of course, she's the strongest warrior in our kingdom—Cat One!)"
Upon hearing her name, the large blue cat emerged from the group and cheerfully waved her paw at Amber.
"Meow. (Then, I'll join.)"
Amber answered without hesitation.
"Meow, meow meow. (Good. From now on, you can call me Boss Cat.)"
Around here, the Cat Kingdom Troupe is a vast underground organization of cats.
Cats Two and Three, who Amber had encountered earlier, were also strays picked up by Cat One and raised in the Cat Kingdom's sanctuary for kittens—that cardboard box in the corner, its official name being the "Cardboard Cat Castle."
Nobody had told Amber these strange details, but they came into her head all the same.

Amber shook her head, clearing away these thoughts, and focused on playing with a magical mouse puppet. This was a daily training routine for the warriors of the Cat Kingdom.
"Meow meow, meow. (Don't let your limbs grow rusty.)"
Boss Cat would remind her warriors daily:
"Purr...meow, meow! Meow! Meow meow! (Soon, we'll face a great battle. We shall liberate our brethren at the cafe!)"

That's right. The mission: Liberate the cats inside the cat cafe.
This was Amber's first mission after she joined the Cat Kingdom. It also served as a test given to her by Cat One the big blue cat after she applied to join her team.
Cat Two was already on the team, but Amber was determined to not fall behind Cat Three, who only ever thought about games. She swatted her paw against the mouse's tail and watched with satisfaction as her ranking rose above Cat Three's.
To her surprise, that single act seemed to change more than just the ranking.
The entire training ground began to shake. Above the field, a giant, featureless human face appeared.
"Purr! Meow meow, meow! Purr! (No! The humans from the cafe have acted first—we've been discovered!)"
Boss Cat's voice came to her from afar.

Amber's eyes flew open, her heart pounding as she glanced up at the sky—
No human face.
And she...was not a cat.
After all, Amber knew how cats communicate—with glances, scents, and body language instead of "talking" back and forth with meows.
Only humans spoke.

"The Cat Kingdom Troupe, the Cardboard Cat Castle, Cats One, Two, Three, and Boss Cat... Was it all just a dream?"
The starlight had no answer for Amber. She closed her eyes, hoping she'd find out the truth someday.

[CharacterArchiveContent.10313.1]
Weapon Anecdote: Cat Treat-1911 Dual-Wield

[CharacterArchiveContent.10313.2]
Amber remembers the name of these guns: Cat Treat-1911.
However, she has no idea where they came from.

It seemed that one day, when she woke up, they were already there—spotlessly clean and neatly placed by her pillow.
"Cat Treat-1911," Amber read out the unfamiliar name as if she'd always known it.
Loading, aiming, pulling the trigger—it all felt so familiar, as if her muscles knew them better than her mind.

"I feel like I've forgotten how I came to have these pistols."
After the formation of the New Star Guild, Amber said this to her companions.
"Knowing they're yours is enough, isn't it?"
The blonde girl answered. Then she pulled Amber away to watch a rare sight—the Shrubshrew migration.

"It looks like they are made by NGO. Look, the bullets are actually filled with Neo Solution... Oh by the way, whatever you do, don't carry these guns openly in the cities. If someone from the Grace Imperium see them, you'll get fined."
The girl warned Amber repeatedly with a most grave expression on her face. She didn't stop until Amber promised not to take the guns out.

Later, a new member joined New Star Guild.
Clutching her pistols, Amber asked the same question to the newcomer.
The amnesiac newbie was surprisingly at home with the grip and aim of these guns. If not for the inability to use magic, they could undoubtedly become an excellent sharpshooter.
But even the "Boss," who was an expert on Artifacts, wasn't sure if this pair of guns was one of them.

Amber didn't let this get her down.
Because she'd already found a clue that will lead her to the truth—
On the day she met the newcomer, inside Emor City's Ash District market, she'd spotted a mark that resembled the bullet holes made by her Cat Treat-1911.
