[CharacterArchiveContent.11211.1]
The Legend of the Twin Stars

[CharacterArchiveContent.11211.2]
"The Legend of the Twin Stars" was a story written by an anonymous author published in Trekker Association's"Trekker's Story Collection: Year █, Iter Month Edition."
It's Noya's favorite story. Perhaps it's because one of the main characters' dreams truly spoke to her.

Here's an excerpt:

Flowers, applause, and dazzling flashes of light.
On the stage of the medal award ceremony, Galavera almost teared up from the constant flashes. She glanced at Daria, who was standing next to her, with admiration. Her partner was calmly reciting her speech without skipping a beat.

Daria knew this would happen, so she only assigned Galavera one simple task: stand to the side, keep your back straight, and smile.
"Of course, don't just stand there like a statue. Hmm, let me think... I'll leave the end of speech to you. You're great at doing mic drops."
Daria leaned down and smoothed the creases out their dresses, making final adjustments before they went on stage.

"Shouldn't I prepare a something in advance?" In her mind's eye, Galavera could see the audience at the ceremony—esteemed board members of the companies and organizations sitting in velvet chairs, potential big clients to the guild.
"What if I get carried away and end up saying something like I want to pluck the stars out from the sky up there again? I'm always told to not say far-fetched stuff like that because it makes us look unprofessional, then the big business in the Gold District will want nothing to do with us."
"But Galavera, this dream of yours sounds so romantic. I really like it." Her partner didn't see the problem. "If the captain asks, just say it was from my draft."
"I'm not gonna have you get blamed for something I said. Hehe, I already know what I'll say!"

Daria's speech was finally coming to its conclusion. The memory stopped.
"Of course, our goal doesn't stop here," Daria smiled as she passed the mic to her partner.
Galavera straightened her back even more. The flashes were on her again. She held back tears and said loudly, clearly, without the microphone. Even those sitting in the back row could hear her every word.
"We will be plucking the stars out from the sky next."

The awkward silence lasted a second before the applause broke out.
The company's directors made the gesture of clapping, if only to maintain their usual formal decorum.
The award ceremony came to an end.

Galavera let out a happy whistled. She'd be dealing with the captain's fury later, after they got off the stage.
Worst case scenario, she'd get fined again. No, actually, Daria would probably get fined this time, too.

[CharacterArchiveContent.11212.1]
The Fall of the Twin Stars

[CharacterArchiveContent.11212.2]
"The Fall of the Twin Stars" was published in the Trekker Association's"Trekker's Story Collection: Year █, Gelu Month Edition." It was written by an anonymous author.
It is the final installment of The Legend of the Twin Stars series as published by the Collection.
Noya stayed up all night finish reading it, then she asked Iris to write a letter to the editorial team to protest the ending that befell the two main characters.

Here is an excerpt:
Galavera licked her pale lips. The stones beneath her feet had caught her unaware; she staggered and had to use her sword to steady herself. Daria, who she was carrying on her back, wasn't so lucky. Galavera's stumble had made she slip a little.
Galavera paused to adjust her stance. She had to make sure that the unconscious Daria wouldn't get injured again.

She had no idea how long they'd been walking.
A seasoned Trekker would always try to minimize her presence inside the Monolith, avoiding unnecessary combat with the Stellaroid.
It was a race against time.
She realized the cloth on her back had grown increasingly wet and sticky, a mixture of her own sweat and Daria's blood.

She's still breathing. Don't panic.
Galavera forced herself to stay calm. She felt her partner's heartbeat—weak, slow, hanging on by a thin thread.
The wind inside the Monolith carried death's chill, swirling around the two Trekkers.

"Mm..."
A soft groan came from behind her. To Galavera's ears, it sounded like thunder! A sign of life!
"Go to the right... There's an Aqua Glopo... You must... stay hydrated..."
Even though she could barely string a whole sentence together, Daria was still dutifully guiding her partner's way.

"You know that no matter how badly I'm injured, I won't die from it! A few weeks of rest and I'll be fine!!"
Galavera heard a faint reply by her ear. Daria couldn't keep the pain out from her heavy breathing. "Mm, but sometimes the body just reacts by itself..."
"Daria, don't fall asleep. Talk to me."
"Your back is too stiff... No way I can fall asleep..."
"Daria... Daria!!"

Three days later, Galavera carried Daria out of the Monolith, but they had paid a heavy price.
Daria had lost one of her legs.

The Twin Stars shone but for a brief moment above Torina before they fell to earth.

Weapon Tale: Noname

[CharacterArchiveContent.11213.2]
Legend has it that it was a long sword entirely in emerald green.
Someone picked this sword up and, with it, ushered in a new era. 
The actress walked out of the magical elevator and said to her companions waiting outside:
"At last, the mysterious box has been opened... I can tell, this marks the beginning of a new era! The gods have heard our wish."

The performance on stage was coming to its climax.
Out of the three girls in the audience, only two were still awake.
One girl reached out and touched Noya's sword.
"Noya's sword looks exactly like the one from the play. You don't think it could really be the legendary sword?"
"Noya bought it at a blacksmith's shop yesterday."
"So she just bought the same one. Come think of it, she still hasn't named this sword. Do you think naming it would bring good luck?"

"Noname."
"Huh Noya, you're awake?"
"No. She's talking in her sleep."
That's how this seemingly ordinary long sword got its name.
