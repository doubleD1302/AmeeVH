The Determination

[CharacterArchiveContent.13311.2]
It happened before Nazuka had made up her mind to form a hunting party.
...
"Hey, long time no see, Lady Nazuka. Out here surveying monster activity in the forest again?"
"... You know me?"
"Of course. I see you often. Always busy from dawn till dusk, rescuing people from rampaging monsters... Your name's been spreading fast lately, you know?"
The stranger introduced himself.
"You saved me, and I came to thank you. Here is my token of gratitude."
"No, there's no need. Keep it for yourself. Times have been hard for Trekkers lately."
"Haha." He gave a weary smile. "That's true. That's why I also came to say goodbye. I'm leaving Philae to try my luck elsewhere."
"Ah..."
"Our last hunt failed. The camp has been run over by monsters again and again... A Trekker at my level just can't get by in Philae anymore."
Nazuka stayed silent.
"Goodbye, Lady Nazuka. You're not like me. You are strong and unwavering. I truly hope you'll succeed."

Nazuka didn't try to stop him. But on that day, she remembered why she had wanted to become a Trekker in the first place—and she knew, at last, what she needed to do next.

[CharacterArchiveContent.13313.1]
Weapon Anecdote: Timeless Grace

[CharacterArchiveContent.13313.2]
After careful thought and consideration, Nazuka chose the staff as her weapon.

Of course, she had a natural gift for large-scale magic, but that was only the foundation. If she wanted, she could have chosen a sword, a bow, or a spear instead. She simply picked the one weapon that best suited her way of fighting.
The windmill head design of her staff was a beautiful coincidence—it reminded her of the past. She never had any particular hobbies; her mind was always on how to improve the lives of Trekkers. She was an idealist who always looked forward.

Even so, there was one thing that truly fascinated her: she loved to watch the endless turning of windmills and waterwheels. In her hometown Philae, the City of Water, that sight was everywhere, and to her, it was the most beautiful of all.
