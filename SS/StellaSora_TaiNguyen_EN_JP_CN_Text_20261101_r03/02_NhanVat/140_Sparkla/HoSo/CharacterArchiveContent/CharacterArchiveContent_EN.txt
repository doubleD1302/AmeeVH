The Bubble Princess

[CharacterArchiveContent.14011.2]
"Far out in the ocean, the water is as blue as the prettiest cornflowers and as clear as the purest glass. But it is terribly deep..."
"... The Ocean King had six beautiful daughters. The youngest princess loved hearing stories about the human world above more than anything else..."

Her mother's soft voice was slowly lulling little Sparkla to sleep. Still, the girl fought to keep her eyes open, slurring her words as she pressed on. "Mommy, is the youngest princess pretty? And ... why does she like the human world so much?"
"Of course she is. The book says her skin was soft as a rose petal, and her eyes as clear as the deep blue sea."
"As for why she liked the human world? Probably because it's so, so far away from her home. It's only natural for her to be curious."
"Just like you, my sweetheart. Aren't you always wondering if giant whales live up in the sky? Hehe."
"Then... Then I hope the youngest princess gets a chance ... to visit the land someday... Zzz... Zzz..."
Before her mother could whisper goodnight, little Sparkla had already drifted off into a deep slumber.

"Waaah... Why did the little mermaid trade her voice just to become human? And... And her feet hurt so badly..."
The next day, Sparkla pestered her mother to finish the story, only to be struck by a devastating plot twist.
Although the little mermaid was finally a part of the human world, she paid a terrible price for it.
"Oh my, why are we crying?" Her mother rushed to comfort her, but nothing could stop the tears rolling down the little girl's cheeks.
Sparkla sniffled. "No, we can't let the little mermaid suffer like that. What if she didn't have to walk? Or ... what if she could live on land feeling completely free? Mommy, I want to build a little moving chair for her! Oh, wait! What about a giant moving fish tank...?" Muttering ideas to herself, she haphazardly scrubbed the tears from her face and sprinted eagerly back into the house.
"Hey now... Go wash your face first..." Her mother held back a chuckle, not the least bit upset. She simply slipped a bookmark onto the page. Seeing her daughter bounce back to her energetic self was all that mattered.

[CharacterArchiveContent.14012.1]
Alicia's Underground Adventure

[CharacterArchiveContent.14012.2]
While organizing her bookshelf, Sparkla noticed a bookmark lying on the floor.
It featured a drawing of an unfamiliar ocean, with a bright splash of color breaking the surface that looked exactly like a fish's tail.
A memory struck Sparkla—this was the bookmark her mother had made for her when she was little. She remembered wanting to build a moving chair for the little mermaid back then, never imagining she'd eventually be the one using it. Of course, basic mobility wasn't enough for her; she had tricked it out with plenty of upgrades. As if on cue, two mechanical arms extended from the back of her wheelchair, nimbly plucking a book from the very top shelf.

"Let's go with this one today. The stall owner said it's from a fresh batch of fairy tales recently unearthed from the Monolith."
"Let's see here... Alicia's Underground Adventure?"
"Alicia was sitting by the riverbank when she suddenly spotted a white rabbit in a waistcoat scurrying past with a pocket watch. Stranger still, the rabbit was muttering to itself, 'Oh dear! Oh dear! I shall be late!'"
Sparkla eagerly flipped the page, only to be completely captivated by the illustration. Oh dear! Oh dear! This Mr. Rabbit, dressed to the nines in a suit and top hat with eyes sparkling like rubies, looked incredibly similar to the snow rabbit her father had built for her years ago!
I have to invent something for him. Anything to help him escape his personal hell of perpetual tardiness!

So, in her dreams that night, Sparkla found herself with a suited rabbit of her very own, who whisked her away to the Six O'Clock Tech Exchange.
It was the exact convention she had always dreamed of—no loudmouthed Leona, no troublemaking Bloc. Just a lovely cat lady and an enthusiastic puppy plushie. In this peaceful haven, she could finally focus on modifying a scooter exclusively for Mr. Rabbit, seamlessly blending high-speed traversal with a built-in baking function.
When she woke up, Sparkla spent a long while basking in the afterglow of her sweet dream. Staring at her empty bedside, a sudden stroke of genius hit her: the suited rabbit from the story would look perfectly at home right here in her room.
For the next month, Sparkla's workshop abruptly halted all incoming commissions. As for how to explain this sudden hiatus to her clients? Well, she'd just leave that headache to Bloc.

[CharacterArchiveContent.14013.1]
Weapon Anecdote: Rabbit Pie

[CharacterArchiveContent.14013.2]
Exactly a month after Sparkla stopped taking orders, Bloc and Leona showed up at her workshop.
"What is your deal?! You take a whole month off, and all your orders are now my responsibility?! I've been doing the work of two and haven't slept in four days... My brain is squeezed dry of inspiration!"
The moment they walked in, Leona drifted toward Sparkla's room like a zombie. "I'm gonna crash for five... Aah! What the heck is that?!"
Bloc peeked in from behind her. "Oh? Is this what you've been working on all month? What do you call it?"
"Watch your pronouns. It's a 'she.' She is my butler, Rabbit Pie." Sparkla hauled Leona out of the room and lovingly adjusted Rabbit Pie's hat. Leona's terrified flailing had knocked it completely askew, as it dangles at the robot's neck. "I guess she's passably cute, but that name is a tad tacky," Leona remarked with a hint of disdain. Still, she couldn't resist running a curious hand over the mechanical rabbit. "Wow, you hid this joint mechanism pretty well..."
"Hands off. Did you just mock my naming? Rabbit Pie, put Leona on the blacklist. She is permanently banned from my room."
Rabbit Pie gave a slow, deliberate nod.
"Hey, hey, you tyrant!" Following its master's orders, the mechanical rabbit gave Leona absolutely no room to argue, forcefully shoving her several meters down the hall.
Leona sulked on the sidelines for a moment before her eyes suddenly narrowed. She just realized something crucial. "Wait a minute... You didn't manually input any commands just now! No way... Does this thing already have voice recognition?! That's insane... Quick, walk me through your entire design process!"

Seeing that the other two had completely forgotten she even existed and scurried straight to the invention room, Bloc simply made herself at home. She expertly retrieved a bag of tea she had left in the cabinet last time and began brewing herself a pot.
"My, my, I can already picture a blissful future where I fall asleep on a giant pile of Dorra... Ohohoho..."
