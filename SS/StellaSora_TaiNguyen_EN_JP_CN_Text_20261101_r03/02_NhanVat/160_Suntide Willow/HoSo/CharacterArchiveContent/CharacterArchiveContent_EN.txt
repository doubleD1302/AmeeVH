The Desk Trinket

[CharacterArchiveContent.16011.2]
A small fry trinket once sat on Willow's desk.

No bigger than a palm, it occupied the top right corner and completely clashed with the rest of her tidy setup.
Yet Willow never bothered to move it. It just stayed there, eventually gathering a thin layer of dust.
Naturally, this sparked some office gossip. Did the trinket have a secret backstory? Was it a special gift, or a memento from an unforgettable day? The fry theme even led to rumors about a certain girl from Grace Imperium who regularly sneaked in through Willow's window, always bringing fresh fries with her.
Could this little knickknack be a token of their unique friendship—

"Willow! Willow! Terrible news! I lost that little fry souvenir I won in the raffle!!"
Her voice reached the office before she did. Willow looked up, finding Laru perched on the windowsill, looking absolutely crushed.
"I tore my house apart yesterday and spent all day searching my office today, but no sign of it at all!"
Willow looked at her, then glanced at the dusty trinket on the corner of her desk.
"You mean this one?"
"Huh?" Laru followed Willow's gaze and immediately hopped down from the window. "Whoa! I left it here?! You should've told me! I've been looking for it for two whole days!"

And just like that, the fry trinket vanished from Willow's desk. But a few days later, a miniature fishing rod toy appeared in that exact spot—and stayed there for the next six months.
Only this time, no one bothered to guess why it was there.

Haggling

[CharacterArchiveContent.16012.2]
Willow and Iris locked eyes at the entrance to the crowded market, both radiating the exact same fierce resolve.
"We are not going a single cent over budget today."
"And we're scoring the absolute best deals!"
They bumped fists and plunged into the bustling market, dead set on walking away victorious.

But let's rewind...
A few days ago, after finalizing the new quarter's shopping list, the Trekkers' House staff realized with horror that the bare essentials alone would blow their entire budget out of the water.
The finance team spent all night crunching numbers and pulling their hair out until it finally hit them: Horizon had recently advanced internal funds for a shipbuilding project. They had an investor lined up, sure, but the Dorra hadn't actually cleared yet.
"Willow, what are we gonna do?! The Trekkers' House is practically out of toilet paper!"
The frantic staffer burst in right as Willow and Iris were chatting about the weekend beach market. After getting up to speed, Iris grabbed the list, gave it a quick once-over, and said, genuinely puzzled:
"But covering all this with your current budget is a total piece of cake! Leave it to me—stretching a Dorra is my superpower."

By evening, the two returned, arms practically overflowing with bags.
Tallying up the receipts, Willow shook her head in awe. "Incredible. We actually have money to spare. I seriously need you to teach me how to haggle like that."
"No way! The only reason we scored such crazy deals today was all thanks to you!"
"But I don't remember contributing?"
"Didn't you notice? You're always looking out for these vendors! The second you asked for a lower price, they were thrilled to give us a discount. That was all you!"
"Now that you mention it..."
Picturing their little market "battles" and recalling the eager, friendly voices of the vendors, Willow broke into a heartfelt smile.
"I guess I never imagined I'd be on the receiving end of everyone's kindness for a change..."

Maybe all the hard work she put in would quietly find its way back to her in different forms.

Weapon Anecdote: Warning Smile

[CharacterArchiveContent.16013.2]
The package from Sparkla held more than just a mechanical arm. She had also slipped in a white megaphone.

Sparkla was notorious for her oddball inventions, so Willow didn't bat an eye. But when she gave the megaphone a try, its sheer volume genuinely impressed her. She figured it would be perfect for the beach—loud enough to shock reckless Trekkers out of diving into dangerous waters.
But Willow should have known better. Sparkla wasn't the type to hand over a standard-issue megaphone.
The gadget's first trick was gushing out a sea of bubbles, convincing Willow it was just a glorified toy. That is, until she fumbled a button one day. The bubbles instantly turned into explosive cannonballs, narrowly avoiding a complete catastrophe.

"Bloc! Don't you ever send me something this dangerous again!"
"Huh? But you said you were at the beach and needed a megaphone!"
"But this isn't a normal megaphone, is it? Why didn't you warn me it doubles as a weapon?!"
"Alright, alright, take it easy. I was afraid you wouldn't figure it out, so I had Sparkla put together a manual for it."
"... You had a manual this whole time, and you're only telling me now?"
"Well, you never asked, so it slipped my mind!"
Willow took a deep breath, actively fighting the urge to strangle the shady merchant. In the end, she gave Bloc a "warning smile" of her own and sent her packing.
