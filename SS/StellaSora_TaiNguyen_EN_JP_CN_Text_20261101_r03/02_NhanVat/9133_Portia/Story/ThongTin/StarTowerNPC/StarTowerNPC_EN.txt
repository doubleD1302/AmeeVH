Portia

[StarTowerNPC.9133.2]
In the convenience stores scattered throughout the Monolith, you'll always find Portia, who often sits lazily behind the counter, dark circles under her eyes from lack of sleep, responding to customers' requests with a half-hearted tone.
It's not difficult to strike up a conversation with her, despite her usual unenthusiasm. Mention "the world beyond the Monolith," and you'll see the subtle wobble of expectation and curiosity in her eyes. Maybe she's really interested in knowing more about it?
