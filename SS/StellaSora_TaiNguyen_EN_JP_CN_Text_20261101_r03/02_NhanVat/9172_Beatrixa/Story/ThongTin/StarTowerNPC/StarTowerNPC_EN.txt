Beatrixa

[StarTowerNPC.9172.2]
When a battle ends, you'll find Beatrixa in front of the stairs with her medical kit. She is the very "Angel of the Monolith" that people talk about.
She never starts a conversation, so many Trekkers never even notice her. She always wears a cold expression and sounds like she's unhappy that you're hurt. Yet, regardless of her attitude, she always heals every Trekker's injuries and reminds them to stay safe. So, maybe she's actually a great caretaker?
