Bernina

[StarTowerNPC.9173.2]
One of the two mysterious "Demons of the Monolith."
They always appear before resting Trekkers and talk to them.
Bernina always holds a lantern in her hand, which can expose a Trekker's deepest desires.
With threats and temptations, she often succeeds in persuading Trekkers to give up what they have in exchange of she offers.
