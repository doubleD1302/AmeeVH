Virigia

[StarTowerNPC.9174.2]
One of the two mysterious "Demons of the Monolith."
They always appear before resting Trekkers and talk to them.
Virigia always holds a mirror in her hand, which can reveal a Trekker's wildest dreams.
With alluring hints and promises, she often succeeds in persuading Trekkers to give up what they have in exchange of she offers.
